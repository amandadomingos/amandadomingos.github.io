01 — About

Olá, tudo bem? :)

I am a Postdoctoral Research and Policy Associate for the Lemann Foundation Programme at the Blavatnik School of Government, University of Oxford. I hold a Ph.D. in Political Science from the Federal University of Pernambuco (Recife, Brazil).

My research sits at the intersection of public policy, subnational and multilevel dynamics, and legislative studies. I am especially interested in the conditions under which politics can drive collective outcomes in policy, with Latin America — and Brazilian municipalities in particular — as my primary region of interest.

I also work on transparency and reproducibility in social science. I am a catalyst for the Berkeley Initiative for Transparency in the Social Sciences (BITSS) and a member of the Teaching Integrity in Empirical Research (TIER) Network.
