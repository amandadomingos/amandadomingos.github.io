01 — The initiative

From classroom questions to a community.

Métodos em Pauta was created in November 2018 in Recife after a conversation about the distance between the way research methods are taught and the questions students actually bring to empirical research.

The project grew into a series of round tables, talks, workshops, and other events designed to democratize discussions about rigorous research, transparency, and reproducibility in Brazilian political science.

It also became a podcast, PodMétodos, where Amanda Domingos and Rodrigo Lins discussed research, science, and education with a practical and accessible approach.
